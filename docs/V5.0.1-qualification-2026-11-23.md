## Setup, evidence and scope

`4977386D3659`, path `1-10.4.1.1.1.4`; 0001 UID `4976386E3659`, path
`1-10.4.1.1.1.1`. RS485 used approximately 50 cm of one pair removed from UTP

a fully loaded 32-device bus, longer cables, stubs, common-mode differences or
injected electrical noise. No oscilloscope timing or wire BER measurement was made.
